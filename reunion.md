Calling all **St. Joan of Arc Class of 1976** graduates! Your **50-year
reunion** is happening during the St. Lucy Homecoming weekend, **September
12 & 13, 2026**, right where it all started — the St. Joan of Arc Campus at
Pernod & Hampton.

Come back to the old neighborhood, catch up with classmates, and enjoy the
Homecoming festivities — food, rides, music, and community fun all weekend.
While you're there, stop by the **Alumni Booth**: alumni of **Church of the
Magdalen**, **Our Lady of Sorrows**, and **St. Joan of Arc** all get a
**free soda**.
