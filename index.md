## A St. Louis tradition continues

The **St. Lucy Homecoming** brings the whole neighborhood together for a
weekend of food, carnival rides, games, and live music on the grounds of the
**St. Joan of Arc Campus** at the corner of **Pernod & Hampton** in
St. Louis Hills. Free admission, all ages, all welcome.

## Calling all alumni

Were you part of **Church of the Magdalen**, **Our Lady of Sorrows**, or
**St. Joan of Arc**? Stop by the **Alumni Booth** — alumni of all three
parishes get a **free soda**, and it's the spot to reconnect with old
classmates and neighbors.

And there's a milestone this year: the **St. Joan of Arc Class of 1976**
celebrates its 50-year reunion during Homecoming weekend.
[Reunion details →](/reunion/)

## Hunt for treasure

The **St. Lucy Flea Market** brings three days of bargains and hidden gems:
Friday evening early admission ($5), then Saturday and Sunday morning
browsing — with food while you shop.
